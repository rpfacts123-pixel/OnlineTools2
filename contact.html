How can we help?

The most useful messages include the name of the tool, what you were trying to do, and what happened. This helps us understand the issue and fix it quickly.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or confidential information in your message.

  • Report broken or confusing tool behavior
  • Suggest a new utility or improvement
  • Ask a question about a calculation or feature
  • Share feedback on usability or website layout

Quick answers

Do I need to include a URL? If the issue is page-specific, adding the link helps a lot.
What if I have a feature idea? Tell us what problem you want the tool to solve and what would make it easier to use.
Is this website only for developers? Not at all. We keep the tools practical for creators, students, marketers, and everyday busy users.
