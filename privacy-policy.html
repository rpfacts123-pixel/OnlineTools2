Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Online Tools & Converters provides online utilities, converters, calculators, and other digital tools. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website or use our tools, including features that rely on external services or artificial intelligence.

1. Information We May Process

The information involved depends on which page or tool you use. It may include:

  • Information you provide: text, file contents, form entries, URLs, prompts, or other information you enter into a tool.
  • Technical information: your browser type, device information, IP address, requested pages, timestamps, and other information that may be recorded by website hosting or security services.
  • Messages you send: information included when you contact us or submit feedback.

2. How Online Tools Handle Your Information

Different tools work in different ways. Some tools may process information directly inside your browser. In those cases, the relevant processing may occur on your device.

Other tools may send information to a server to perform a requested operation. Do not assume that all tools work locally or that information entered into every tool stays on your device.

Before using a tool, consider whether the information you provide is sensitive. Avoid entering passwords, payment details, private documents, confidential business information, or personal information about others unless necessary and appropriate.

3. AI-Powered Features

Some features, such as an AI-powered metadata generator, may send information you enter to our server-side endpoint so that an AI service can generate a response.

When you use these features, the submitted text, prompts, video details, or other required inputs may be processed by our infrastructure and relevant service providers, including Cloudflare Workers AI where that service is used.

We cannot promise that AI-related inputs are processed only on your device. The handling, retention, and use of information by an external provider are also subject to that provider's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

AI-generated responses may be inaccurate or incomplete. Review generated content before publishing it or using it for important decisions.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or the services used to operate it may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies for functions such as remembering settings, maintaining security, measuring usage, or supporting optional third-party features.

The technologies used can vary as the website develops. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

5. Hosting, Security, and Third-Party Services

Our website uses hosting and infrastructure services, including Cloudflare for website delivery and server-side functionality. These providers may process technical information or information needed to deliver the service.

If we add analytics services, advertising providers, embedded content, or other third-party integrations, those services may collect information in accordance with their own policies. Please review the privacy policies of relevant third-party services when using their features.

6. How Information May Be Used

Information may be processed, where applicable, to:

  • Provide the tool or feature you requested.
  • Operate, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the website.
  • Respond to questions, support requests, and feedback.
  • Detect misuse, abuse, or activity that may threaten security.
  • Understand usage and improve website functionality where appropriate.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not publish information you submit to a tool simply because you used that tool. However, information may be handled by infrastructure providers or other services when necessary to deliver the requested functionality, maintain security, or meet legal requirements.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, or operation of the website and its users.

8. Data Retention and Security

Retention depends on how a particular tool and its service providers operate. Information processed in your browser may remain in that browser until it is cleared or removed. Server logs, messages, or information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the relevant systems and policies.

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the website's operation to protect information. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection to processing, or other aspects of your personal information.

You can also choose not to provide optional information, stop using a tool, or adjust your browser's cookie settings. Some functions may not work if required information or technologies are unavailable.

To raise a privacy question or request, please use our Contact page.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is not specifically directed at children. Anyone using the website should avoid submitting unnecessary personal or confidential information. If you believe a child has submitted personal information and you have a privacy concern, please contact us through the Contact page.

11. International Processing

Because the website relies on internet infrastructure and may use third-party providers, information may be processed in countries other than your own. The protections and rules that apply may differ between countries.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our tools, infrastructure, or practices change, or when updates are needed to address legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date where appropriate.

13. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or concerns about information handled through the website, visit our Contact page.
